## Science notes (what the picture claims)

- Neutron star = the collapsed core of a massive star after a supernova; progenitors
  roughly 8–20× the Sun's mass.
- ~20 km across, typically ~1.4× the Sun's mass (more mass than the Sun).
- One teaspoon of neutron-star matter would weigh on the order of a billion tons on
  Earth — shown as a **density comparison** (on-screen: "A DENSITY COMPARISON. NOT A
  SHIPPING PLAN."). The film never claims it can be brought to Earth; the final joke
  is literally that it can't.
- Atoms are mostly empty space; in a neutron star, electrons and protons are crushed
  into neutrons packed shoulder to shoulder.
- Pulsars: beams from the magnetic poles sweep space as the star spins; the fastest
  known spins ~716 times per second ("700+"). Beams are drawn stylised, not literal.
- "*not to scale" is flagged on screen where the neutron star is drawn tiny.
