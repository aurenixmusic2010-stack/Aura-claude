# Storyboard: The Metal Slowly Killing Wolverine

1080×1920 · 30 fps · 1530 frames · style: **MISFEED** (see `style/misfeed/README.md`).

The look: newsprint, riso inks, misregistered plates, the page slipping on the
glass. Real Marvel art is die-cut with a paper border and lifts off the page,
casting a shadow; that is the "coming out of the page" move. Comic-book effects
are kept to almost nothing: no speed lines, no SFX lettering.

**Ink rules (fixed for the whole episode):** toner `#121417` for metal, shadow
and type; amber `#F2A33A` means healing; green `#9BE15D` means poison; scan
blue `#2640FF` exists only inside a fault (the page slipping, Magneto's pull).

**Safe zones:** the main subjects stay between y 180 and y 1380. Captions sit at
y≈1450, above the Shorts title area; the right edge stays clear of the
button column.
